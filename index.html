The Routine

Warm up. Loosen up. Lock in.

Three steps. Sixty seconds. Then wrap your hands, tie your laces, and get to work.

1

Shake

Shake the bottle until the formula is frothy. That is the sign the active botanicals are evenly suspended and ready to go.

2

Apply

Spray onto the body areas you are about to load. For the strongest warm-up effect, apply after a brief sweat or a warm shower.

3

Get to Work

Wear your kit a minute after applying. Non-greasy. Non-staining. Out of your eyes when you sweat. Re-apply between rounds as needed.

Questions, Answered

Before you add to bag.

What is Athlon Rub, exactly?
A next-generation Thai-oil topical liniment. FDA-registered as a cosmetic. Built on the heritage of authentic Muay Thai liniment and reformulated in the U.S. for better viscosity, absorption, and scent. Used before training to warm up the body, and as part of a recovery and massage regimen after.
Will it transfer to my gear?
No. Athlon Rub is fast-absorbing and non-greasy. You can put your kit on a minute after applying. It does not sit on the skin like petroleum-based balms and does not stain compression gear or wraps.
What about banned substances and testing?
Athlon Rub has historically carried Tested Clean third-party certifications — most recently Informed Choice, and earlier the more rigorous batch-by-batch Informed Sport program. Active certification is currently under evaluation as part of the re-launch. The formulation itself has not changed. If you are an athlete subject to drug testing and need a current letter of certification before purchase, contact us before adding to bag.
Where is it made?
In an FDA & ISO Certified Lab in the United States. Athlon Rub is not a contract-manufactured Thai import — it is formulated and produced domestically under a regulated cosmetic-product registration.
How does the warming sensation work?
The formulation produces a clean warming sensation calibrated for athletes who sweat hard and open their pores fully. The heat scales with your body temperature, not with raw irritation — so it intensifies once you start to move, rather than burning before you have warmed up.
What if I don't like it?
First-time buyers are backed by a money-back guarantee. If Athlon Rub does not fit your routine, you get your money back. It is the bottle we want in your bag — not on a regret list.
